Who is the residency for?

Artists with real records and a real decision to make about 2027: what to release, who you are to a listener, where to put your time and money. If you are still finishing your first songs, come to the masterclass.

What do I leave the residency with?

Your personalized 2027 Artist Blueprint, the recordings from your two sessions, and a follow-up consultation after the weekend. The Blueprint is a structured plan personalized to you, with a six-month release roadmap and a 90-day action plan.

What is included in the recording sessions?

Twelve hours of studio time, six in each room, with a Torch engineer. Production, beats, mixing and mastering are not included, and no number of finished songs is guaranteed. Arrive with your beats and ideas ready and you will get the most from the time. You own what you record.

How does residency payment work?

Accepted artists pay a 50% deposit within 48 hours to secure the suite. The balance is due December 8. Artists accepted in the final days before that date pay in full. Payment is by card through Square.

How does the VIP consultation work?

At checkout you submit one record, your profiles and your biggest career challenge. Your private 15-minute A&R consultation is held by video call the week after the masterclass, so it is prepared and not rushed. You pick your time from a link sent after the event.

Where is Torch?

A private creative estate in metro Atlanta. The address and gate instructions go to confirmed residents and ticket holders by email 48 hours before.

Refunds and transfers?

Masterclass tickets are non-refundable. You can transfer a ticket to another artist by emailing admin@torchatl.com before December 12. Residency deposits are non-refundable once your suite is secured. If Torch cancels or moves the event, you choose a full refund or the new date.
