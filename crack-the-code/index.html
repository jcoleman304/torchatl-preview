The Concept

The artist is the case study.

You arrive with the three records you believe best represent who you are right now. We pressure-test the music and the artist, find the gap between what you intend and what the music actually says, and turn the diagnosis into a plan you can execute in 30 days.

This is not a feedback panel. It is a development methodology, run by Torch, on Torch's property.

Who is this for?

Artists with finished records and a real decision to make: what to lead with, who you are to a listener, what to do next. If you are still finishing your first songs, come to the Mixer and apply next edition.

What do I submit?

Three records you believe best represent who you are right now. Streaming links, or private SoundCloud or Drive links for unreleased music.

Is the pre-work really mandatory?

Yes. The session is built from your answers. If pre-work is not in by the deadline, the seat is released and converted to Mixer access plus a credit toward the next edition. No cash refund.

What does the Residency include?

Two nights on site at Torch (Friday and Saturday), welcome dinner, Sunday breakfast, all sessions, 3–4 dedicated studio hours with an engineer, the Saturday mixer, and your documented 30-day plan.

Where is Torch?

A private creative estate in metro Atlanta. The address and gate instructions go to confirmed artists and ticket holders 48 hours before.

Refunds?

Residency and Private Session payments are non-refundable once pre-work has been sent. Mixer tickets are transferable; email admin@torchatl.com.

Are Torch members priced differently?

Members get first review on applications. Mixer tickets are $25 for members at the door with a valid membership. Residency and Private Session pricing is the same for everyone.
