Welcome

Torch ATL is not a recording studio.

It’s a gated creative estate.

Two industry-standard recording suites inside a luxury property built like a five-star retreat. A chef’s kitchen, lounges, an outdoor deck, guest suites, and the presidential suite.

Torch was designed as a private estate for serious creators, where the room is ready before you arrive and the people in the kitchen between sessions are the collaborators, managers, and A&Rs to connect and expand your network. Membership is capped, and admission is by application.
