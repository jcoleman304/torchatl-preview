Welcome

Torch ATL is not a recording studio.

It’s a gated creative estate.

Two industry-standard studios, a Strategy Room, and a Content Wall inside a luxury property built like a five-star retreat. A chef’s kitchen, lounges, an outdoor deck, Guest Suites, and The Torch Suite.

Torch was designed as a private estate for serious creators, where the room is ready before you arrive and the people in the kitchen between sessions are the collaborators, managers, and A&Rs to connect and expand your network. Membership is capped at twenty seats, and admission is by application. Members get studio hours every month and a member rate on everything else the estate offers. Membership is open to artists, producers, writers, podcasters, creators, and brand studios, not only musicians.

Questions

Asked and answered.

How do the hours work?

Every month your account loads with ten hours. Spark hours are for the sets and for podcast or voice audio in a studio; Flame and Blaze hours are for Studio B and Studio C. One hour is one hour, any time of day. Sessions are three hours or longer, so most members use their hours across two or three sessions.

What if I don’t use all my hours?

They roll into the next month. Unused hours carry for one month and then expire. Need more time instead? Members can add hours at the member rate.

When can I book?

Monday through Saturday, any open slot on the calendar. Every member books up to seven days ahead. Camps and whole-estate bookings are scheduled first, and members fill the open time around them. On the rare occasion a camp needs a slot you already hold, we give you at least 48 hours’ notice, return your hours, and help you rebook.

Which membership is for me?

Spark is for content: podcasts, streams, shoots, and batching days on the sets. Flame and Blaze are for music in the studios, and the difference between those two is the engineer. Flame is the room and the hours: bring your own engineer, or book one of ours when you need one. Blaze includes a Torch engineer on every session, with a deeper member rate across the estate.

What does it cost?

Rates are shared privately after your tour, along with your terms. We keep them off the website on purpose.

What if I need to cancel a session?

Cancel at least 24 hours ahead and the hours return to your account. Inside 24 hours the room was held for you, so the hours are used.

How hard is it to leave?

Not hard. Three-month minimum, then month to month. No exit fees and no long-term contract.

Who can I bring to a session?

Up to three collaborators join any session at no charge: writers, producers, your engineer, your manager. The estate is theirs for the session too.

Does membership include staying overnight?

No. Stays, camps, and estate days are booked separately, and members get the member rate on them.

What’s the tour actually like?

Forty-five minutes walking the estate with us. No pitch and no pressure. Afterward we let you know where things stand, and if it’s a fit on both sides, you can be booking sessions the same day.
