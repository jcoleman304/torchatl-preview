For Labels & Management

How label teams run Torch month over month — for A&R admin, A&Rs, label presidents, managers, and product teams.

A developing act that records every week doesn't need a new quote every session. Torch runs standing monthly accounts: a block of Studio Credits on account, drawn down by the roster, billed as one predictable line item.

Monthly accounts
Blaze — $3,000/month for 44 Studio Credits; built for album mode and producer rosters. Torch Executive — $4,500/month for 60 Studio Credits, with two roster artists on the account, priority booking, and preferred pricing across the estate. Credits book in four-hour blocks; up to a quarter of unused credits roll to the next month. Seats are capped. Application at torchatl.com/membership.
Development Partnerships
For rosters beyond membership scale: recurring residency agreements with priority access and dedicated studio time, scoped per roster. Partnerships begin where the Torch Executive tier ends.
Admin & billing
No membership is required for label bookings — hourly sessions ($80–$140/hr by suite and engineer option, four-hour minimum), buyouts and stays book directly on a PO. Booking requests carry label name, A&R contact, and PO number fields. One invoice per booking — or per month on account. NDAs standard for staff and sessions.
Rollout & content
Content wall, podcast-ready booth, camera-ready interiors — product managers and streamers run shoot days, listening sessions, and live broadcasts on the same property where the record is made.

Logistics & Policies

The advance block. What a tour manager or label coordinator needs before anyone gets in a vehicle.

Access
Gated single-entry drive; the address is never published — released on confirmed booking only. 24-hour session access for the duration of the stay: no meter, no midnight engineer curfew. Check-in 3:00 PM, early on request · check-out 11:00 AM, late if the schedule permits. Overtime is approved through management before the scheduled end time.
Occupancy
Six overnight guests across three suites; up to twelve with lounge space at the booking team's discretion. Each studio: 5 people included, 7 maximum, entering with the primary client. Event capacity is quoted per event.
Parking
Gated on-site parking for up to 15 vehicles — Sprinters and SUVs. Security-detail friendly: one perimeter to hold instead of three a day.
Privacy
NDAs available for staff and sessions. Guest registration on every entry; visitors are pre-cleared by the client's team before arrival. No location tagging — protecting unreleased records is a house rule. No public listing of guests or sessions. Tinted privacy windows — see out, no one sees in.
Cameras
Camera-monitored grounds — exterior, entry points, hallways, and common lounges only. No cameras in studio rooms, guest suites, or bathrooms.
Content
Photo and video capture is permitted across interiors and grounds; clients own what they shoot. Photoshoot wall on-site; house photographer available.
Smoking
Smoking scope is communicated per booking. Cigarettes are not permitted inside; the deck carries a designated smoking area. Violations carry a remediation fee starting at $300.
Noise
Outdoor levels are kept down late night. Studio sessions are permitted to run around the clock on Estate Lockouts.

Frequently Asked

The short answers. Anything not covered here is answered within 24 hours at bookings@torchatl.com.

Is another client ever on property during a booking?

Never during Estate Buyouts, Creative Estate Lockouts, or Private Events — those bookings receive exclusive access. During hourly studio sessions, both suites may run at once, so another artist may be recording in the other suite.

Is smoking permitted?

Smoking scope is communicated per booking. Cigarettes are not permitted inside; the deck carries a designated smoking area.

Can sessions run overnight?

Studio sessions run around the clock during Estate Lockouts. Hourly sessions book by reservation with a four-hour minimum.

How many guests can stay overnight?

Six across the three king suites; up to twelve with lounge space, at the booking team's discretion.

Do we need a membership to book?

No. The estate books by reservation; members receive preferred rates and priority booking, and label teams book directly on a PO.

Can we bring our own engineer?

Yes.

Is catering available?

A private chef and catering are arranged in advance, beginning at $300/day. The chef's-grade kitchen is included on estate bookings.

Can we shoot content on property?

Yes — photo and video across interiors and grounds, and clients own what they shoot. No location tagging; protecting unreleased records is a house rule.

When is the address released?

On confirmed booking only. The estate is gated, unlisted, and never published.

Are pets permitted?

Trained dogs only. Scope is communicated per booking and must be approved in advance, at an additional fee.
