Questions before we get started

How does the night balance work on the Camp Account?
Fifteen nights are prepaid on one invoice. Each confirmed stay reduces the balance, and Torch sends an updated balance after every stay. Nights are redeemable for 12 months from the agreement effective date.
Can different artists on our roster use the account?
Yes. The account belongs to the label, not one artist. Use nights across multiple roster artists for 3-night camps, 5-night lock-ins, longer stays, or one continuous residency. Multiple artists and creatives can also come at the same time: both studios run simultaneously and the estate sleeps up to 10. One booking contact on your side, one concierge on ours.
How much notice do you need for a stay?
Seven days preferred, three days minimum. Shorter-notice requests are welcome when the estate and staff are available. Each reservation is a minimum of three consecutive nights.
Is the estate exclusively ours during a stay?
Yes. Exclusive estate use during confirmed stays: both recording suites, all three guest suites, lounges, meeting room, content wall and grounds, with up to ten overnight guests.
What is included, and what is quoted separately?
Included: two Torch engineers at 8 hours each per day, on-site security, a named concierge, and turnover cleaning. Quoted separately and approved before booking: additional engineering hours, extended security, private chef and catering, transportation, content days and photoshoots.
How do we pay?
Both packages are prepaid. One PO and one invoice. Dates are confirmed in your agreement, which also sets guest limits, studio access hours, cancellation terms and applicable taxes.

Anything else? Email bookings@torchatl.com and we will answer within one business day.
