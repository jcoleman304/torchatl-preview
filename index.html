About

Where You
Feel At Home

You pull through the gate and the city disappears. The phone goes quiet. The only deadline is the one you set for yourself. By the second hour you've forgotten what building you're in — and that's the point.

Torch ATL is a private estate in Atlanta for creatives who demand more than four walls and a console. No commercial building. No strangers. No shared hallways. Just the grounds, the rooms, and whatever you came here to make.

For artists, writers and producers, it's a place to disappear into the work — fully staffed, fully private, built around the session, not around the clock. For labels and publishers running camps, it's a turnkey creative setup without the logistics, the leaks, or the compromises.

You don't book a room. You get the whole estate.

Membership

Twenty-Seven Keys.

Torch Membership is a capped, application-only seat at the estate. Four tiers — from eight staffed studio hours a month to a label-grade account with roster privileges. Every membership includes the house: both studios, the chef's kitchen, the deck, the grounds, and a Torch house engineer on every session.

Beyond the booth — members-only listening nights, industry mixers, masterclasses, and monthly A&R office hours. Members book first, at member rates, on everything the estate offers. When the twenty-seventh key is claimed, the doors close.

Ten founding memberships exist. When the tenth signs, founding terms never return.
