Membership

Twelve Keys.

Torch Membership is a capped, application-only seat at the estate. Four tiers — from eight Estate Credits a month to a label-grade account with roster privileges. Credits spend across Studio B, Studio C, the Strategy Room, and the Content Wall, and every membership includes the house: the chef's kitchen, the lounges, the deck, and the grounds — at preferred rates, with priority booking. Membership is open to artists, producers, writers, podcasters, creators, and brand studios, not only musicians.

Beyond the booth — members-only listening nights, industry mixers, masterclasses, and monthly A&R office hours. Hourly block sessions in the studios are a member and partner benefit. Members book first, at member rates, on everything the estate offers. When the twelfth key is claimed, the doors close.

The first six members sign on founding terms — rate locked for 24 months, permanent founding designation. When the sixth signs, those terms never return.

What is Torch ATL?

Torch ATL is a private creative estate on the east side of Atlanta. Two professional studios, three guest suites, lounges, a chef’s kitchen, a Strategy Room, and a Content Wall sit behind one gate on 5,021 square feet. Labels run album lock-ins and writing camps here, brands shoot here, teams go off-site here, artists and executives stay here, and creators go live here.

Does Torch ATL have overnight accommodations?

Yes. Torch ATL has three guest suites, The Torch Suite, King I, and King II, each with its own bathroom, and sleeps up to ten overnight. Overnight is included with Estate Lockouts, which have a three-night minimum, and with residencies. Estate Days are daytime only.

Can Torch ATL host a writing camp or label camp?

Yes. Writing camps, label camps, album lock-ins, and retreats run at Torch ATL as Estate Lockouts. Both studios run at the same time, all three suites house the roster on property, and the kitchen, lounges, and grounds are reserved for one client for the length of the stay.

Where is Torch ATL located?

Torch ATL is a gated estate on the east side of metro Atlanta, about twenty-five minutes from Hartsfield-Jackson and twenty minutes from Downtown. The street address is never published. It is released only on a confirmed booking, which is how sessions stay private.

How is Torch ATL different from a commercial recording studio in Atlanta?

Most Atlanta studios rent rooms by the hour inside a commercial building. Torch ATL is a whole private estate. On Estate Days and lockouts the studios, suites, kitchen, and grounds are reserved for one client at a time, with no shared hallways and no other sessions. It is built for productions, hosted gatherings, and multi-day stays, not walk-ins.

What gear is in the Torch ATL studios?

Studio B runs a Neumann U87 through a Neve 1073 SPX and a Tube-Tech CL1B into a UAD Apollo X6, with Shure broadcast mics for podcast and livestream audio. Studio C runs a Neumann TLM 103 through a UA LA-610 MkII into a UAD Apollo X8. Both rooms run Pro Tools with Yamaha HS8 monitoring, and a house engineer is available on every session.

Create

Can we shoot on the grounds and interiors as a location?

Yes. The interiors, grounds, deck, kitchen, suites, and the Content Wall’s three backdrops are all available as set on an Estate Day or a multi-day production. Lighting is bring-your-own or a rentable add-on.

Can brands bring their own production crew?

Yes. Crew, talent, and agency teams work on a closed set with gated parking for fifteen vehicles. A Torch coordinator is on property, and the house engineer is optional.

What is the upload speed, and is ethernet wired to each room?

Torch ATL runs a dedicated connection with wired ethernet in the production rooms, and the network is not shared with other guests during a private booking. We confirm the current speed-test result and which rooms are wired when we quote a livestream or upload-heavy production.

Gather

What is the capacity for a seated dinner or a standing gathering?

Gatherings are sized to the format and the rooms you use: the chef’s kitchen, the Executive Lounge, the Strategy Room, and the deck. Tell us seated or standing and the headcount, and we confirm the layout in your quote. Overnight is capped at ten guests.

Is there private A/V for presentations?

Yes. The Strategy Room and the lounges are set for presentations and screenings, and an A/V and livestream package is available as an add-on.

How does catering work?

A private chef and catering are on call, from $300 a day. The chef’s kitchen, the deck, and the grill are included with every estate reservation, so your team can also self-cater.

Stay

Can a team do a multi-day off-site with overnights?

Yes. A three-night Estate Lockout gives a team the Strategy Room, both lounges, the kitchen, the deck, and all three guest suites, with 24-hour access and a dedicated coordinator.

How many can sleep on property?

Up to ten guests across three guest suites and lounge space. Every suite has its own bathroom, and housekeeping and turnover are included on multi-day stays.

Is the address public?

No. The address is disclosed on confirmation only. The estate is gated, with tinted privacy windows, fifteen-car gated parking, no shared hallways, and security available on request.

How do I book Torch ATL?

Send a request through torchatl.com, email bookings@torchatl.com, or call (404) 800-9507. Every request is reviewed personally, then you receive a tailored quote. A 50% deposit and the estate agreement confirm your dates. Estate Days, lockouts, hosted events, and residencies are booked by request; hourly block sessions in the studios are a member and partner benefit.
